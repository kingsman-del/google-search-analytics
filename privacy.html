1. Scope and current status

Search Intelligence is a personally developed research and analytics project intended to analyze estimated Google keyword search demand, brand and competitor trends, and geographic patterns. This policy covers the public informational website and explains how a future authorized Google Ads API integration is intended to operate.

Current status: The public website is a static demonstration site hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not currently provide public account creation, Google Sign-In, a Google Ads account connection, or live Google Ads API data extraction. The developer is configuring a separate Python/OAuth integration. Functionality described below as planned is not a claim that it is already operational.

2. Information collected through the website today

The current static website does not contain a registration form, analytics tracking script, advertising pixel, or a form requesting Google account credentials. It does not intentionally collect visitors' names or email addresses through the page itself.

If you contact the developer by email, the information you choose to send (such as your email address, message, and attachments) is received through the email provider and used to answer or manage your inquiry. The website's hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times, browser details, and error or security logs under GitHub's own policies. Search Intelligence does not independently control those hosting logs.

3. Google account access and Google Ads API data

At present, no Google user data is collected through an active Google OAuth integration on this website.

If Google Ads API access is approved and the Python integration is activated, the application is intended to request authorization from a Google account with access to a Google Ads account. The proposed Google Ads OAuth permission is the Google Ads API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords. This permission can be broad; Search Intelligence intends to use it for authorized, read-only keyword research workflows, not to create campaigns, modify ads, or change advertising budgets. The developer will not request passwords and will use Google's OAuth authorization process.

Subject to authorization, the planned integration may process the Google Ads customer ID; account accessibility information required to identify the selected authorized account; selected keyword text; estimated average and historical monthly search volumes; supported geographic and language targets; keyword competition indicators and bid estimates; timestamps; and API status or error messages. OAuth authorization may also involve access tokens and refresh tokens used to maintain access. Search Intelligence does not intend to request Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Contacts, or unrelated Google account information.

Keyword search-volume figures are estimates supplied by Google and are not the identities or search histories of individual Google Search users.

4. Purposes of use

Information is used or, for the planned integration, will be used only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to emails and support or privacy requests.
  • To authenticate authorized access to Google Ads using OAuth and retrieve requested historical keyword metrics.
  • To prepare personal research analyses of brand, competitor, category and regional search demand.
  • To organize authorized metrics into local CSV/Excel files, quality-control checks, visualizations, and historical comparisons.
  • To diagnose technical errors, protect the application from misuse, and comply with legal obligations where applicable.

Google user data will not be used for unrelated advertising personalization, profiling of individuals, credit decisions, or sale to data brokers. Any material change in purpose will require an updated notice and, when required, renewed consent before new processing begins.

5. Storage and security

Currently, the public website contains only static files and does not have an application database for visitor accounts or Google Ads data. Contact emails are handled within the developer's email account and its provider.

For the planned Python integration, the intended design is to keep OAuth secrets and tokens outside the public GitHub repository, restrict their access to the developer, use HTTPS/TLS for API communications, and store keyword results in controlled local files or an access-restricted database. Credentials should not be included in public reports. These controls will be checked before any live integration is enabled. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure; suspected unauthorized access can be reported to the contact email below.

6. Sharing and disclosure

Search Intelligence does not sell personal information or Google user data. The current website is hosted by GitHub Pages; email communication is processed by the email service used by the developer. If activated, the planned integration will communicate with Google APIs to obtain authorized data and may use a hosting or storage provider only where necessary for the stated analytics functions.

Google OAuth tokens and account-specific data will not be publicly posted to the website or repository. Data will not be shared with unrelated third parties for their marketing or analytics purposes. Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law or necessary to investigate security incidents, limited to what is reasonably necessary. If the project's sharing practices change, this policy will be updated before such processing begins.

7. Retention and deletion

Website contact messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed to handle correspondence, troubleshoot issues or meet applicable legal obligations, and may then be deleted. Hosting logs follow the hosting provider's retention practices.

Once the Google Ads integration is operational, authorization tokens will be retained only while needed to maintain authorized access and will be deleted or invalidated when access is disconnected or no longer needed, subject to technical and legal limitations. Exported keyword metrics may be retained for longitudinal trend analysis; account identifiers and other account-linked data will be retained only as necessary for the approved project purpose. Data no longer needed will be deleted or de-identified where feasible. These procedures must be implemented and tested before the integration is made available to others.

To ask what data is held, request correction or deletion, or request that a connected account no longer be used, email groverj205@gmail.com with enough information to locate the request. The developer will review and respond within a reasonable period, subject to identity verification and applicable obligations. You can also revoke a Google app's authorization from Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized access; it does not by itself erase previously exported files, so a separate deletion request may be needed.

8. Cookies and third-party services

The current Search Intelligence pages do not set first-party tracking cookies or run a first-party behavioral analytics service. GitHub, your browser, and any links you choose to follow may have independent data practices. If login, analytics, embedded services, or additional cookies are added later, this policy will be updated to describe them and any required consent choices.

9. Your choices and rights

You may choose not to email us, decline a Google OAuth authorization request, or revoke Google authorization at any time. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to obtain information about, access, correct, or delete personal data, or object to certain processing. Send requests to groverj205@gmail.com. Some requests may require verification to prevent unauthorized disclosures.

10. Children

Search Intelligence is intended as a general research and developer project, not as a service directed to children. It does not knowingly seek to collect personal data from children through its website. If you believe such information has been provided, contact the developer for review and deletion where appropriate.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when the actual application's functions, permissions, data storage, or third-party services change. The updated policy and date will be published on this page. Where required, affected users will be notified and asked for permission before new uses of Google user data.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, Google data access questions, correction or deletion requests, or security concerns, contact the developer of Search Intelligence at groverj205@gmail.com.
